Networking & Infra

The physical and logical foundation the cluster sits on. A UniFi stack handles routing, switching, and VLAN segmentation. A UNAS Pro provides NFS storage. A Beelink runs utility VMs on Proxmox. An AREDN node connects to the amateur radio mesh.

UDR7 router 10 VLANs 172.20.Y.Z UNAS Pro

Everything outside the k3s cluster: the physical devices, the network segmentation, DNS resolution, NFS storage, firewall zones, and backup strategy. The cluster is just one server on VLAN 20 — the network around it is where the homelab's operational resilience lives.

02 Physical topology

All servers sit on VLAN 20 (Servers). The UDR7 trunks every VLAN down to the Flex switch, which connects all devices. Wireless clients land on Personal, IoT, or Guest VLANs depending on which SSID they join.
